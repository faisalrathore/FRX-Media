Why Moving Company Ads Need To Separate Move Types

Most moving company marketing runs one generic campaign for "movers" — mixing a $400 local move with a $6,000 interstate relocation in the same funnel. That undervalues long-distance jobs and overspends chasing low-value local leads, because both get the same bid strategy and creative.

Local move searches convert on volume and speed of response. Long-distance and interstate move searches are a higher-consideration purchase with a much larger average job value, and justify a meaningfully higher cost-per-lead — plus a lead-quality focus, since this category is plagued by resold, duplicate leads from national aggregators.

FRX Media builds moving company campaigns split by move type and ticket value, so your business gets steady local move volume while separately targeting the smaller, higher-value audience booking long-distance and interstate relocations — using owned lead generation, not purchased aggregator leads.

FAQ

Questions About Our Moving Company Ads Agency

How much does a moving lead cost through paid ads?+

Local move leads typically cost $15-40 depending on market. Long-distance and interstate move leads run considerably higher, often $50-120+ per lead, because the job value is much larger and the lead pool is more competitive with national lead-resellers.

Should moving companies run ads for local or long-distance moves?+

Both, structured separately. Local move campaigns are built for volume at a lower CPL. Long-distance and interstate campaigns target a smaller, higher-intent audience and can support a higher CPL because average job value is significantly larger — we run these as separate campaigns with different budgets, creative, and often different landing pages.

How do you handle lead quality for moving company ads?+

Moving is an industry with a known lead-reselling problem — the same lead often gets sold to five companies. We focus on owned lead generation through your own Meta and Google campaigns rather than buying from lead aggregators, and build in duplicate and fake-lead filtering on the intake side.

Do you handle FMCSA/USDOT compliance considerations in moving ads?+

We're not a compliance or legal service, but we build ad copy and landing pages that reflect your actual registration status (local vs. interstate authority) and avoid claims that could create compliance issues, in coordination with whatever your company already discloses.

Can you handle multi-location or franchise moving companies?+

Yes. We build location and territory-specific campaigns with geo-targeting and call routing, so leads reach the right branch or crew rather than one generic campaign spread across every location you operate.
