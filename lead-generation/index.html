Why Service Businesses Need a Different Playbook

Your Revenue Happens
After the Lead

An online store gets paid at checkout. You get paid after a phone call, a quote and a site visit. If your agency optimises for the cheapest form fill, Meta finds you the people most likely to fill in forms, not the people most likely to hire you.

How most lead campaigns run
  • Optimise for the cheapest possible lead
  • One-question forms that anyone can submit by accident
  • Leads sit in a spreadsheet for hours before anyone calls
  • Meta never learns which leads actually became customers
  • Reports show CPL, while your sales team says "these leads are junk"
How we run them
  • Optimise for cost per qualified lead and booked appointments
  • Qualifying questions and OTP phone verification filter out low-intent leads

  • Lead outcomes sent back to Meta through the Conversions API

The Lead Quality Loop

From Ad to Booked Job,
Then Back to Meta

This is the part most agencies skip. We don't stop at the lead. We connect what happens next, and we teach the algorithm to find more of the leads that close.

01

Ad & Offer

A clear offer with a reason to act now, plus creative that pre-qualifies by service, area and price range.

02

Qualify

Form questions, OTP verification or a WhatsApp flow screen out tire-kickers before they cost your team time.

03

Instant Follow-up

A real-time CRM or Sheets sync, instant alerts to your team and an auto-reply to the lead within seconds.

04

Track Outcome

Each lead is marked Contacted, Qualified, Booked or Won in your CRM, so we know which ads produce real customers.

05

Feed Back to Meta

Qualified and won leads go back through the Conversions API, so Meta optimises for customers, not form fillers.

5 min

Speed to lead is often worth more than a lower CPL. The same lead is far more likely to book when someone contacts them within minutes rather than hours. That's why every setup we run includes instant alerts and an automated first reply, whether that's WhatsApp, SMS or email.
