Why Garage Door Ads Need To Separate Job Types

Most garage door marketing runs one generic campaign for "garage door service" — mixing a $150 spring repair with a $2,500 full door replacement in the same funnel. That undervalues high-ticket installation jobs and overspends chasing cheap repair calls, because both get the same bid strategy and creative.

Emergency repair searches (broken spring, stuck door, dead opener) are high-urgency and convert fast on a call-focused landing page. Installation and replacement searches are more considered and justify a higher cost-per-lead because the job value is much larger — treating them identically wastes budget on one side or starves the other.

FRX Media builds garage door campaigns split by job type and ticket value, so your business gets steady emergency-repair call volume while separately targeting the smaller, higher-intent audience shopping for a new door or full replacement.

FAQ

Questions About Our Garage Door Ads Agency

How much does a garage door repair lead cost through paid ads?+

Emergency repair leads (broken springs, off-track doors, dead openers) typically cost $15-40 depending on market competitiveness, since these are high-urgency, high-intent searches. New installation and full door replacement leads run higher, usually $40-90, because the job value is significantly larger.

Should garage door companies run ads for repairs or new installations?+

Both, structured separately. Emergency repair campaigns target high-urgency searches ("garage door won't open", "broken spring repair") with fast-loading call-focused landing pages. Installation and replacement campaigns target higher-consideration searches and can support a higher CPL because the average ticket is much larger.

Do you use Google Local Services Ads for garage door companies?+

Yes, where available in your market. Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed) perform well for emergency home-service categories like garage door repair because they lead with reviews, licensing, and a phone call — exactly what a homeowner wants when a door is stuck.

How fast do garage door companies see leads from paid ads?+

Emergency repair campaigns typically generate call volume within days of launch since the search intent is immediate. Installation and replacement campaigns take slightly longer to stabilize, usually 2-3 weeks, as we test creative and offer angles for a more considered purchase.

Can you handle multi-truck or multi-territory garage door companies?+

Yes. We build territory-specific campaigns with geo-targeting and call routing so leads reach the right crew or location, rather than one generic campaign spread across every service area you cover.
