Why The Right Campaign Mix Beats Either Approach Alone

Most agencies pick a side — either running everything through Advantage+ Shopping or sticking with fully manual campaigns. Both approaches leave performance on the table in sports and outdoor, where prospecting and retargeting genuinely need different tools to work well.

Advantage+ Shopping excels at cold prospecting once creative is proven and purchase history is deep enough for Meta's algorithm to optimize against. But it doesn't segment intent levels precisely — which matters for retargeting, where manual CBO gives the control to treat a cart-abandoner differently from a casual browser.

FRX Media combined both in a real sports and outdoor account — Advantage+ for prospecting, manual CBO for retargeting — and cut cost-per-purchase by 31% versus running either approach alone, delivering 13.28× peak ROAS across 6,150 purchases.

FAQ

Questions About Sports & Outdoor Meta Ads

Should sports and outdoor brands use Advantage+ Shopping or manual campaigns?+

Both, used for different purposes. Advantage+ Shopping excels at cold prospecting once you have proven creative and enough purchase history for Meta's algorithm to optimize against. Manual CBO retargeting gives more control over intent-level segmentation, which Advantage+ doesn't handle as precisely.

How much can Advantage+ and manual CBO combined reduce cost per purchase?+

In a real sports and outdoor account we managed, combining Advantage+ Shopping for prospecting with manual CBO for retargeting reduced cost-per-purchase by 31% compared to running either approach alone.

What creative works best for sports and outdoor gear?+

Action and use-case content — gear being used in the actual outdoor or sport context it's built for — outperforms studio product photography. Durability and performance demonstrations build trust for higher-consideration outdoor equipment purchases.

Does seasonality affect sports and outdoor ad performance?+

Yes, significantly. Categories split between summer outdoor gear, winter sports equipment, and year-round fitness/team sports each have distinct demand calendars. We plan budget and creative around your specific sub-category's seasonal pattern rather than a generic annual calendar.

What ROAS has FRX Media achieved in sports and outdoor?+

FRX Media achieved 13.28x peak ROAS for a sports and outdoor brand across 6,150 purchases, using Advantage+ Shopping for prospecting combined with manual CBO retargeting — reducing cost-per-purchase by 31% versus either approach alone.
